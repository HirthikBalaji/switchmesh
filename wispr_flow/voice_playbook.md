## 🎙️ Executive Summary: Why Voice Changes Multi-Agent Chat

In high-pressure production incidents (Track 1) or fast-flow creative game jams (Track 2), typing complex markdown instructions into chat is a bottleneck. 

With **Wispr Flow**, we transformed human-agent collaboration into an ultra-low latency, natural voice interface:
1. **On-Call Voice Triage & Hands-Free Dual-Custody Approval**: The engineer speaks into Wispr Flow: *"Approved. Roll back cart-service to version two thirteen point two and verify error rate drops to zero."*
2. **Indie Game Direction at the Speed of Thought**: The creator dictates complex game mechanics, Web Audio frequencies, and particle physics without leaving the creative flow state.
3. **Voice-Authored Room Templates & Docs**: All Switch room instructions, agent personas, and documentation were dictated using Wispr Flow Pro.
