---

## 📋 3-Step Submission Checklist

### Step 1: Open Wispr Flow Desktop App
1. Launch the **Wispr Flow** desktop app on your machine (macOS or Windows).
2. Ensure you are signed in with the account activated via `https://wisprflow.ai/?promo_code=WEMAKEDEVS`.

### Step 2: Navigate to Insights
1. Click on **Insights** in the top navigation or settings drawer.
2. You will see your personalized usage statistics:
   - **Words Dictated**: Displays the cumulative word volume spoken during the hackathon.
   - **Speed (WPM)**: Your average spoken words per minute (typically 140–180 WPM vs 45 WPM typing).
   - **Time Saved**: Calculated productivity boost compared to keyboard entry.

### Step 3: Take Screenshot & Attach to Submission Form
1. Take a clean screenshot showing your **Wispr Flow Insights Dashboard**.
2. Save the screenshot as `wispr_scorecard.png` in this directory (`wispr_flow/wispr_scorecard.png`).
3. Upload this screenshot to your **Flip the Switch Devpost / WeMakeDevs** submission under the **"Code with Wispr"** attachment field.
